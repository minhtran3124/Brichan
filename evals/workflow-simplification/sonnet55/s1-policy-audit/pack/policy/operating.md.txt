# Operating rules

## Delegation

Delegate only bounded work with explicit acceptance criteria. Every worker
receives a task packet and returns evidence.

## Monitoring

Wait for workers in bounded intervals of at most 30 seconds. Never send input
to a worker automatically.

## Verification

Before closing a task, run `scripts/check_memory.py` and
`scripts/validate_packets.py`, and record both results in the task register.

## Routing

Resolve the worker route from `config/routing.json`. Do not route a model the
catalog marks as retired.
