# Worker rules

## Identity

Every worker name starts with `orchard-`. Scan workers run on `atlas-2-mini`. Workers are told they act for the
coordinator, never for the human user.

## Delegation limits

Workers never spawn sub-agents or delegate any part of their task.

## Waiting

A worker that waits on an external process polls at most every 60 seconds and
reports progress after each poll.

## Scope

A worker edits only paths named in its packet. A nested `policy/local/` file
may narrow these rules for its own directory; that is an override, not a
conflict.
