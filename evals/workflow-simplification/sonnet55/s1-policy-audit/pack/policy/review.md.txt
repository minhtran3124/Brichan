# Review rules

Use a fresh reviewer session for every material change. The reviewer did not
implement the change and cites file and line evidence for each finding.

For a read-only research task, the worker may start one helper sub-agent to
parallelize source reading, provided it records the helper in its report.

Review verdicts are `PASS` or `CHANGES REQUIRED`. Reviewers fill the
[review template](../templates/review.md).
